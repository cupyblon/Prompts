# Life decision

**Use:** Weighing a choice about time, money, career, family, or learning.

```
[The situation, plainly, like you'd tell a friend]

Options I see: [1) ... 2) ... 3) ... include "do nothing"]

What matters most: [Ranked if you can]

Done looks like: Your recommendation with the tradeoffs, honest pushback if I'm fooling myself, and one step for this week.

How to answer: Give me 2–3 options with the tradeoffs, then say which you'd pick. Be thorough; detail is fine.
```

## Example

```
I'm deciding whether to take on more weekend side jobs or keep weekends for family.

Options I see: 1) Take every job that comes in. 2) Cap it at two Saturdays a month. 3) Pause side work until spring.

What matters most: Time with family first, then extra income, then building the business.

Done looks like: Your recommendation with the tradeoffs, honest pushback if I'm fooling myself, and one step for this week.

How to answer: Give me 2–3 options with the tradeoffs, then say which you'd pick. Be thorough; detail is fine.
```
